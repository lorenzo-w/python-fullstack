# How to choose an AI model

There are many good, open-source model options for coding assistance nowadays. It is a good idea to have a quick look at some leaderboards to see what models are available and how they perform, but don't spend too much time, as context and fine-tuning tends to be more important than the exact foundation model:

- [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/): Aggregated formal benchmarks with custom scoring
- [llm-arena](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/text/coding): Preference ratings by human users
- [onyx](https://onyx.app/llm-leaderboard): Leaderboard aggregated as tier list for simple choosing
- [open-router rankings](https://openrouter.ai/rankings): Raning based on usage statistics
